Photographs behind the marketing pages. Each one is saved twice: `-red.webp`, the picture mapped into reds for the home hero and the closing bands, and `-gray.webp`, a grayscale for the photo panels, the steps band and the page heroes. The washes that sit over them are the `.photo-*` classes in `src/app/globals.css`.

Sources, as supplied on 2026-11-17:

- `weld-*`: a 540×360 preview from Adobe Stock (file 219023764), enlarged to 1600 wide.
- `parts-room-*`: a 612×459 preview from iStock (file 2227932164), enlarged to 1600 wide.
- `machine-shop-*`: a photograph of a CNC shop, 2000×1500.
- `late-models-*`: `late_model_hof-1.jpg`, a late model collection, 2560×1600.

Both stock images are licensed (confirmed 2026-11-17).
